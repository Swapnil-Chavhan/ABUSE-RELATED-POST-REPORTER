About us

There have been many kinds of abuse cases reported every year on this platform. Some of them are: - child abuse, women abuse, etc. Many times, victims do not report to the police station or concerning authorities directly abuse is not limited only to humans, it is also done with animals, which cannot report it to anyone. So, nothing is done to solve these issues. So, we got an idea to create a platform where such cases can be reported to the concerned authorities. So that these issues can be solved and more and more victims can get justice.

The idea is to create a website where a keyword can be entered and then the data scraped from several websites (where the data related to that keyword can be obtained) and will provide it on the same single webpage. So that, the authorities will not have to spend time searching over different sites to find such reports. Social media websites can be considered best for such cases as many people nowadays use hashtags to report such cases, in a hope that the concerned authorities will check these posts. So, our website has used such two main platforms, i.e., Instagram and Twitter. A web scraper is a piece of software that automates the time-consuming process of extracting valuable information from third-party websites.
